    How does scoring work?

    Every sunset starts with a score of 30. High and mid clouds catch the sunset's color. About 50% coverage is ideal; too few clouds will be plain, too many clouds will just be gray. With this, we calculate the canvas quality, which can receive a score as high as 70. If there are low clouds or rain, points are subtracted from the total score.
